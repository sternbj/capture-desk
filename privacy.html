Capture Desk data use

Capture and storage

Capture Desk records the display, window, tab or rectangle you select. Camera and microphone media are included only when enabled. Recordings and screenshots are stored in your browser profile. You can download them or grant the Chrome extension access to a folder you choose. If you pick a synced folder, its sync service handles that copy. Browser controls and settings are saved locally.

Optional AI requests

When you run an AI action, Capture Desk sends the image or text you selected and your prompt to OpenAI, Anthropic or OpenRouter, or to your configured local Ollama instance. OpenRouter can route a request to a model provider. Your key is submitted only to the selected service and kept in the open assistant dialog. Each service has its own terms and data handling. You can use recording, editing and downloads without AI.

Accounts and telemetry

The public Capture Desk builds require no Capture Desk account and contain no analytics or advertising code. The site hosting the static app can receive ordinary page requests.

Browser extension permissions

Capture Desk uses browser capture permissions to provide the features you request. The Chrome Web Store version uses data for its stated recording, editing and optional analysis features in accordance with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
